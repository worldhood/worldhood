# Community

| Where | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/worldhood/worldhood/issues)** | Bugs, the [wishlist](IDEAS.md), area claims, and coordinating work on places and the engine |
| **[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/worldhood/worldhood/discussions)** | Questions, design ideas, show-and-tell; searchable and permanent |
| **[Discord](https://discord.gg/c6SaPvxhJG)** | Live chat, screenshots, city threads and build-alongs |

Decisions are recorded on GitHub (issues, pull requests, discussions), not only in chat. See
[GOVERNANCE.md](../GOVERNANCE.md) for who decides and the quality bar.

## The Discord server

Kept small on purpose. Each city gets a thread in #city-builders rather than its own channel,
until it has someone looking after it.

```
START HERE
  #welcome          read-only: what worldhood is, the two ways to help, ground rules
  #announcements    read-only: releases, new cities, events
COMMUNITY
  #general          hang out, say hi
  #screenshots      drives, walks and rides (include the ?city=&start= link)
  #ideas            gameplay ideas and what-ifs, before they become issues
  #help             stuck, or found a bug
BUILD
  #city-builders    building and improving cities; one thread per city
  #gameplay         prototyping what you do in worldhood, plus engine and performance
  🔊 build-along     voice, for building together
```

| Role | Who | Can |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maintainer | Project and area maintainers | Post in read-only channels, moderate, see #mod-log |
| Contributor | Anyone with a merged pull request | Coloured name, as a thank-you |

**Ground rules** (pinned in #welcome): be kind (we follow the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md));
nothing that mocks or targets real people, private homes or sensitive sites; use open data and photos
you're allowed to use, and credit them; decisions live on GitHub; post with care (screenshots with the
place's link, bug reports with steps, cities aiming for the Helsinki bar); no spam or self-promotion.

**Moderation:** members need a verified email; AutoMod blocks slurs, sexual content, spam, mass
mentions and invite links to other servers, and reports to a private #mod-log channel.

The server's channels, roles and pinned messages are set up by a script that can be re-run, so
changes are made there rather than by hand. Ask a maintainer if something should change.
